Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

I want to cancel my order
We'll do our best to help you cancel your order. Please note, orders can only be cancelled before they have been accepted by the restaurant. You can check the status on the generic Tracking page.
How do I apply a coupon code?
In your Cart, below the Cart items, you'll see a 'Bill Details' section. Enter a valid coupon code (like WELCOME50) in the input box and click Apply.
Do you charge for delivery?
Yes, we charge a nominal ₹40 delivery fee for most areas and a fixed ₹5 platform fee, but we occasionally provide free delivery coupons!
My payment failed but money was deducted
Don't worry! Sometimes bank servers face glitches. Any deducted amount for a failed transaction will automatically hit your account within 5-7 business days.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

We understand sometimes you might change your mind. Here is our policy regarding ongoing order cancellations and payment refunds.

Cancellation Rules

  • You can cancel your order within 1 minute of placing it without any cancellation fee.
  • If the restaurant has already started preparing your food, the order cannot be cancelled and no refund will be issued.
  • If the delivery partner is at your doorstep but you are unavailable, the order is cancelled with a 100% cancellation fee.

Refund Process

If your order is cancelled by us due to an unavailable item or unserviceable location, we will issue a full 100% refund. The refund process generally takes 3-5 business days to surface in your original payment method depending on your bank.
